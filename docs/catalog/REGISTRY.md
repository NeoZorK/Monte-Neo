# Public registry

Evaluations of catalog strategies are recorded in the public
[Trading Strategy Registry](https://github.com/Protocolith/trading-strategy-registry), on a tamper-evident
[Protocolith](https://github.com/Protocolith/protocolith) ledger.

- Monte-Neo writes: verdict, ARL level and depth, rules version, certificate id (`monte-neo export`, planned for v0.56).
- Protocolith stores: the pre-registration before the validation run, then the result, hash-chained and anchored in time.
- The registry shows: entries by category, ARL badges, and the strategies that failed.

Status: under construction. The record schema is a draft; no records yet. Not investment advice.
