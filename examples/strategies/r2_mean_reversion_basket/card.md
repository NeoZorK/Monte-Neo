# Short-term mean reversion basket, long-short, 18 coins

**Round 2** of the Strategy Lab (pre-registered before any run).

> Not investment advice. A research example for testing a verification pipeline, not a call to trade or a promise of profit. Simulated results do not predict future returns.

## The idea

Score = minus the 5-day return of the coin; long the three-day-ahead losers, short the winners, hedge the beta with BTC; rebalanced every 3 days.

## Why it might work

Short-term overreaction and liquidity provision effects reverse within days in many markets.

## Where it breaks

Turnover is high so costs dominate (the cost-doubling column matters most here); trends in crypto can run for weeks and flatten the strategy. Survivorship: the 18 coins are today's liquid survivors, which flatters history (the verifier warns about it); coins that died in 2018-2022 are not in the table.

## Fixed before looking at data

lookback 5, horizon 3 days (grid: lookback 3/5/10). Costs (round 2): Binance taker fees by tier (default VIP0: spot 10 bps, perpetual 5 bps) plus 2-12 bps spread/slippage per coin (BTC 2, ETH 3, small coins up to 12), see `_common/binance_costs.py`; every result is also reported with costs doubled. The grid checks only the plateau and every grid point counts as a trial; `n_trials` of the certificates is the number of variants declared anywhere in the Lab.

## How it is tested

Discovery 2017-08 to 2020, validation 2021 to 2024 (bull, bear and recovery), sealed holdout 2025 on (see `strategy_lab/README.md`). The holdout is opened once, only for a candidate frozen in a git commit. A failure is published as a reviewed case; nothing is tuned afterwards. Per-regime (calendar-year) results, the share of return explained by BTC and the cost-doubling Sharpe are in `report.md`.
